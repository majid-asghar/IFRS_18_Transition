# IFRS 18 Transition Brief

Nov 22, 2026 · @MAJID

## Why it matters

IFRS 18 *Presentation and Disclosure in Financial Statements* replaces IAS 1 for annual periods beginning on or after 1 January 2027, and it must be applied retrospectively. A December year-end group therefore restates its 2026 comparatives, so the 2026 numbers need to be capturable in the new format now. Recognition and measurement do not change, but the face of the income statement, the cash flow statement and several notes do. This brief sets out what changes, the timeline, a practical transition plan and the main risks, as the basis for a fuller paper, manual and deck.

## What changes

| Area | What IFRS 18 requires | What it means for a manufacturer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Categories in profit or loss | Every income and expense item goes into one of five categories: operating, investing, financing, income taxes, discontinued operations. | Operating is the default bucket. Returns from associates, JVs and surplus cash usually go to investing; interest on borrowings and lease liabilities goes to financing. |
| New subtotals | Two defined subtotals: operating profit, and profit before financing and income taxes. | Replaces today's self-defined "operating profit" or EBIT lines, so headline numbers can move. |
| Management-defined performance measures (MPMs) | Subtotals used in public communications (for example adjusted EBITDA) are disclosed in one note, reconciled to the nearest IFRS subtotal, with the tax and non-controlling interest effect of each reconciling item. | Investor-deck KPIs come inside the audited accounts, so their definitions need governance. |
| Aggregation and disaggregation | New principles on grouping items by shared characteristics; large "other" lines must be explained or split. If expenses are shown by function, specified natures (depreciation, amortisation, employee benefits, impairments, inventory write-downs) are disclosed in a note. | Cost of sales by function needs a by-nature split from the costing system. |
| Cash flow statement | Indirect method starts from operating profit. For most non-financial entities, interest and dividends received are investing, and interest and dividends paid are financing. | The policy choices allowed under IAS 7 go away, so cash flow lines may reclassify. |

Recognition and measurement are unchanged; the balance sheet sees only limited presentation changes. Source: [IFRS 18 (IASB)](https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/list-of-standards/ifrs-18-presentation-and-disclosure-in-financial-statements/).

## Timeline

For a December year-end, the first IFRS 18 annual report is for 2027, and 2026 is the restated comparative year.

- **April 2024:** IASB issued IFRS 18. Early application is permitted.
- **2026 (now):** comparative year. Capture 2026 data in both IAS 1 and IFRS 18 formats.
- **1 January 2027:** effective date; 2026 comparatives restated retrospectively.
- **First 2027 interim report:** condensed interims under IAS 34 use the IFRS 18 headings and required subtotals, and MPM disclosures apply.
- **2027 annual report:** includes a reconciliation, for the comparative period, of each restated profit or loss line from IAS 1 to IFRS 18.

## Practical transition steps

1. **Impact assessment:** map every P&L and cash flow line to the new categories, and identify the group's main business activities (which decide whether some returns sit in operating).
2. **Chart of accounts and ERP:** add category and nature attributes to GL accounts so the split comes from the system, not spreadsheets.
3. **MPM inventory:** list every performance measure used in investor decks, board packs and press releases; decide which to keep, then define and reconcile them.
4. **Dry run on 2026:** restate the 2026 income statement, cash flow statement and notes, and the opening reconciliation, before the year closes.
5. **Policies and pack:** update accounting manuals, group reporting packs for subsidiaries, and the financial statement template.
6. **Stakeholders:** brief the audit committee, auditors, lenders (covenants defined on EBIT or EBITDA) and analysts on how headline numbers will move.

## Key risks

- **Late comparatives:** 2026 data not captured in the new format makes the restatement a manual, error-prone exercise. Mitigate with the 2026 dry run.
- **Classification judgements:** FX differences, hedging gains, and returns on investments or cash are easy to misclassify. Document each judgement in the policy manual.
- **Moving headline numbers:** reported operating profit changes, which can surprise investors and trip covenant definitions. Model the effect early and talk to lenders.
- **MPM audit exposure:** measures that were informal become audited disclosures. Agree definitions with the auditor before year-end.
- **System and subsidiary gaps:** by-nature data and category tagging must flow from every entity. Test the reporting pack with each plant.
